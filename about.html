About Me

Designing with purpose, precision and creativity.

Haroon Mughal — Graphic Designer, working under the brand haroonmughalgfx. I focus on creating clean, professional and visually effective designs.

haroonmughalgfx

Design work built to actually get used.

I'm a graphic designer focused on creating professional visual solutions rather than simply decorating a page or a logo. My work sits at the intersection of visual clarity and strong composition — typography that's set with intent, spacing that holds together, and files that are genuinely ready for print, not just for screen.

Brand consistency matters as much as the individual piece. Whether it's a logo mark, a vector conversion or a full promotional set, I care about how each asset holds up next to everything else in a client's brand — and about the small details that separate a rough concept from finished, professional artwork.

My Story

How I got here.

I started exploring graphic design because I was interested in how typography, shapes, images and colors could transform a simple idea into something visually powerful.

As I continued learning and experimenting, I became increasingly focused on the technical side of design — composition, typography, color, vector artwork and creating files that are not only visually strong but also practical for real-world use.

Over time, I developed a particular interest in creating clean, professional graphics and converting rough or low-quality artwork into polished, usable designs.

Today, my work focuses on helping clients turn ideas, references and existing artwork into professional visual assets that can be used across digital and print applications.

Understand the goal → Build the visual direction → Refine the details → Deliver polished artwork.
